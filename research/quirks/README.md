# Horse quirks: research results

The **quirk-research workflow** (`research/workflows/horse-quirk-research.js`, 3 Oct 2026) worked through all 152 roster horses in 12 groups. One researcher per group read Japanese Wikipedia, JRA, netkeiba, newspaper columns and English sources. A skeptic then re-fetched every cited page and checked the quote and the value.

- **318 quirks** on **122 horses** survived (verified or adjusted); none were unsupported.
- **143** map onto a build or running-style control, on **72 horses**, and are in `data/quirks.js`.
- **175** fit no control yet (listed below as candidates for new parameters). They still show on the horse card under "Known for".
- Full evidence with quotes: `quirks-verified.json`.

## Candidates for new parameters (quirks that fit no control yet)

- **Darley Arabian**: Coat markings, not body shape. Thomas Darley's 1703 letter describes a bay with an unusually large blaze down the face ('something of the largest') and white on the near fore… ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20121112000657/http://www.bloodlines.net/TB/Bios/DarleyArabian.htm))
- **Godolphin Barb**: Very short back behind deep shoulders that are well laid back. ([source](https://www.tbheritage.com/Portraits/GodolphinArabian.html))
- **Godolphin Barb**: Slightly over at the knees (knees sprung forward), with heavy shoulders. ([source](https://web.archive.org/web/20130305160307/http://www.bloodlines.net/TB/Bios/GodolphinArabian.htm))
- **Saint Lite**: Upright (steep) shoulder [肩は立ってて]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/セントライト))
- **Saint Lite**: Heavy, thudding, ponderous running action ('dosun, dosun') [ドスン、ドスンて走り方], unlike the light, quick Minami More. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/セントライト))
- **Speed Symboli**: Long body and long legs, a typical stayer's build [胴長・脚長という典型的なステイヤーの体型]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/スピードシンボリ))
- **Speed Symboli**: As a foal and yearling he was very long-legged, tall and slender with a shallow chest [胸の薄い細身]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/スピードシンボリ))
- **Speed Symboli**: Habit of raising his head and neck to look around [首をあげて周りを確かめるしぐさ]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/シンボリルドルフ))
- **Speed Symboli**: After the finish he habitually neighed loudly with tears running, as if complaining about being made to race. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/スピードシンボリ))
- **Haiseiko**: Short below the knee (short cannons), which made him look less refined [膝下が短く]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ハイセイコー))
- **Haiseiko**: Huge, short-coupled frame: 171 cm at the withers, body length 163 cm, girth 188 cm, over 650 kg as a stallion. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ハイセイコー))
- **Haiseiko**: Occasionally played with his tongue while on the bit, so he raced in a bit lifter [ハミ吊り] from the Satsuki Sho on. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ハイセイコー))
- **Maruzensky**: Toed-out forelegs [外向肢勢]: below the knee the forelegs bent outward, forming an 'A' seen from the front. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/マルゼンスキー))
- **Katsuragi Ace**: Long-legged, lanky, unimpressive build [脚が長くひょろっとして]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/カツラギエース))
- **Katsuragi Ace**: Wore a custom white hood [白い覆面 / メンコ] in the 1984 Japan Cup, his first time in a hood, with extra-thick leather over the ears to act as earplugs against the crowd. ([source](https://hochi.news/articles/20181121-OHT1T50272.html?page=1))
- **Katsuragi Ace**: Very bit-sensitive and keen to pull forward, so in the Japan Cup he was ridden on white reins about 30 cm longer than normal, to keep the bit off his mouth. ([source](https://hochi.news/articles/20181121-OHT1T50272.html?page=1))
- **Mr. C.B.**: Springy, bouncing 'rubber-ball' action [ゴムまりのような弾むような走り方 / 全身がバネのよう]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ミスターシービー))
- **Mr. C.B.**: His signature tack was a bit lifter [ハミ吊り], the three-pronged device on his face that kept his tongue from going over the bit. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ミスターシービー))
- **Mr. C.B.**: Thin, weak hoof walls [蹄が薄かった]; he raced and trained in aluminium shoes. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ミスターシービー))
- **Mr. C.B.**: Striking, 'human-like' eyes. Jockey Yoshinaga called them his greatest charm and said he never met another horse with eyes like that. The beauty of his eyes was said to pass to… ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ミスターシービー))
- **Symboli Rudolf**: Habit, inherited from grandsire Speed Symboli, of raising his head and neck to check his surroundings [首をあげて周りを確かめるしぐさ]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/シンボリルドルフ))
- **Symboli Rudolf**: Strong lead-leg preference: jockey Okabe said he was 'left-handed' [左利き]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/シンボリルドルフ))
- **Sirius Symboli**: A known kicker who was supposed to wear a red kicker's ribbon on his tail [蹴る可能性を示す赤いリボンを尻尾に]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/シリウスシンボリ))
- **Gold City**: Flaxen chestnut [尾花栗毛] with a golden mane and tail, inherited from his sire Viceregal. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ゴールドシチー))
- **Inari One**: Small, compact, lightweight build that was still very powerful [小柄ながら力も非常に強く / 軽くてコンパクトなボディー]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/イナリワン))
- **Inari One**: Ran with an action that used the whole body, so he did not look small at speed (jockey Masato Shibata) [体全体を使うフォーム]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/イナリワン))

- **Mejiro Ardan**: A big, heavy-framed horse of over 500 kg [500キロを超える大型馬]. ([source](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/f14fdf46b5fa9f6b0218a6fb4cb38e2794ef0932))
- **Oguri Cap**: Extremely strong 'raking' of the track with the forefeet. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/オグリキャップ))
- **Oguri Cap**: Right foreleg toed out from birth [生まれつき外向していた右脚]. ([source](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/オグリキャップ))
